# Toolforge Privacy Policy

**Effective date: September 30, 2026**

Toolforge is an Android utility application developed by **Fernlight**. Toolforge provides tools for processing video, photo, PDF, audio, and other supported files.

This Privacy Policy explains what information Toolforge and the third-party services used by Toolforge may collect, how that information is used, how files are handled, and how you can contact us about privacy matters.

## 1. Information Toolforge Handles

Toolforge does not require you to create an account.

Toolforge is designed as a local-first application. File-processing operations are performed on your device whenever the requested operation supports local processing. Toolforge does not operate a cloud service that uploads your selected files to a Toolforge server for processing.

Toolforge does not intentionally collect or store on its own servers:

* Your name
* Your email address
* Your phone number
* Your contacts
* Your precise location
* Your photographs
* Your videos
* Your audio files
* Your PDFs
* Your documents
* Your archives
* The contents of files that you select for processing

Some third-party services described below may independently process technical, analytics, or purchase-related information when Toolforge uses their services.

## 2. Product Analytics

Toolforge uses **PostHog** to understand how users interact with the application and to improve its functionality.

Toolforge sends limited product-usage events to PostHog. Depending on the event, these may include:

* The Toolforge tool or screen used
* Whether an operation was started, completed, cancelled, or failed
* Processing duration
* Application version
* Subscription or purchase product involved in a purchase event
* A limited error code or error message when a purchase fails
* Other event-specific information necessary for the analytics event

Toolforge's analytics implementation does not intentionally send the contents of your selected files to PostHog.

The PostHog Android SDK may also process technical information and pseudonymous identifiers associated with analytics events. PostHog can associate anonymous events using a pseudonymous identifier; Toolforge does not intentionally provide PostHog with your name, email address, phone number, or other direct identity information.

Toolforge does not use PostHog for advertising or to sell your personal information.

PostHog processes data according to its own privacy and security practices. More information is available in PostHog's privacy documentation.

## 3. Files and Media

When you select a file using Android's file-selection mechanisms, Toolforge receives access to the file necessary to perform the operation you requested.

Depending on the tool, this may include:

* Photos
* Videos
* Audio files
* PDFs
* Documents
* Archives
* Other supported file types

Toolforge processes supported files locally on your device.

Selected files are not intentionally uploaded to Toolforge servers for processing.

Output files are created on your device according to the operation you request. When supported, Android's file picker, storage framework, or sharing mechanisms allow you to choose where output files are saved or shared.

You are responsible for the files you select and for the destinations to which you choose to save or share output files.

## 4. File Metadata

Some Toolforge features read file metadata as part of the requested operation.

For example, metadata tools may display information contained in a file, while metadata-removal tools may remove supported metadata from an output file.

File metadata processed by Toolforge is handled locally on your device and is not intentionally transmitted to Toolforge servers or PostHog.

## 5. Toolforge Pro Purchases

Toolforge offers optional paid features through Google Play.

The available Toolforge Pro products may include:

* Monthly subscription
* Yearly subscription
* Lifetime one-time purchase

Product availability and pricing may vary by country, currency, and Google Play configuration.

Purchases and subscriptions are processed through **Google Play Billing** and **RevenueCat**.

RevenueCat is used by Toolforge to manage purchase information, verify purchases, manage subscription status, and determine whether a user has an active Toolforge Pro entitlement.

RevenueCat may process information associated with a purchase, including purchase history, Google Play purchase information or purchase tokens, transaction information, subscription status, product identifiers, and technical information necessary to provide its service. RevenueCat states that purchase history is required data when its SDK is used with Google Play.

Toolforge does not receive or store your payment card number, bank account credentials, or other payment credentials.

Payment information is handled by Google Play according to Google's applicable privacy policies and terms.

Toolforge receives information necessary to determine whether Toolforge Pro is available for the current user and stores the resulting entitlement state locally on the device so that Pro functionality can be enabled.

## 6. Google Play

Google Play may process information necessary to provide:

* App installation and distribution
* Purchases
* Subscription management
* Payment processing
* Purchase verification
* Refunds
* Fraud prevention
* Other Google Play services

Google's processing of this information is governed by Google's own privacy policies and terms.

Toolforge does not control the information collected or processed directly by Google through Google Play.

## 7. RevenueCat

Toolforge uses RevenueCat as a service provider for in-app purchase and subscription management.

RevenueCat may receive information necessary to verify and manage Toolforge purchases, including purchase history and transaction-related information. RevenueCat also processes technical information required to operate its service.

Toolforge uses RevenueCat's Android SDK and does not intentionally send your selected file contents to RevenueCat.

RevenueCat encrypts data in transit and provides mechanisms for developers to request deletion of customer data.

For more information about RevenueCat's own privacy practices, please consult RevenueCat's current privacy policy.

## 8. Network Access

Toolforge requests the Android `INTERNET` permission.

Network access is required for services such as:

* PostHog product analytics
* Google Play functionality
* RevenueCat purchase and entitlement services

The `INTERNET` permission is not used by Toolforge to upload your selected files for cloud-based processing.

Toolforge does not intentionally transmit the contents of your selected files to PostHog, RevenueCat, or Toolforge servers for processing.

## 9. Local Settings and Usage Information

Toolforge stores certain information locally on your device to provide its functionality.

This may include:

* Theme and appearance preferences
* Color-scheme preferences
* Local usage counters
* Free-tier usage information
* Local Toolforge Pro entitlement state
* Other application preferences required for normal operation

This information is primarily stored locally and is not maintained as a developer-controlled user account.

## 10. Data Sharing

Toolforge does not sell your personal information.

Toolforge does not use advertising SDKs to provide targeted advertising.

Toolforge may transmit limited information to third-party service providers when necessary to operate specific features:

**PostHog** — product analytics and application-usage measurement.

**RevenueCat** — purchase verification, subscription management, and entitlement management.

**Google Play** — application distribution, billing, purchases, subscriptions, transaction processing, and related Google Play services.

These services may independently process information according to their respective privacy policies and service terms.

Toolforge does not intentionally share the contents of files you select for processing with these services.

## 11. Data Retention and Deletion

Toolforge does not maintain a developer-controlled account database containing your files or a personal profile.

Local application settings and usage information remain on your device until they are cleared by Android, cleared by the application, or the application is uninstalled, subject to Android's device and backup behavior.

Files created by Toolforge remain in the storage location you select and can be deleted using Android's file-management functionality.

Purchase and subscription information handled by Google Play and RevenueCat may be retained by those services according to their respective retention policies and legal obligations.

If you want to request deletion of information associated with Toolforge's use of RevenueCat, contact us using the privacy contact below. We may need to use RevenueCat's available deletion mechanisms to process such a request.

## 12. Security

Toolforge is designed to minimize unnecessary transmission of user files by processing supported files locally on the device.

Network communications used by third-party services are handled through their respective SDKs and services.

No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure. You should keep your Android device, operating system, and applications appropriately secured.

## 13. Children's Privacy

Toolforge does not intentionally collect personal information from children and does not create user accounts or child profiles.

If Toolforge's target audience or data practices change, this Privacy Policy will be updated as appropriate.

## 14. Third-Party Services

Toolforge currently uses the following third-party services:

* **Google Play / Google Play Billing** — application distribution and purchases
* **RevenueCat** — purchase verification, subscription management, and entitlement management
* **PostHog** — product analytics

Each service may process information under its own privacy policy and terms.
