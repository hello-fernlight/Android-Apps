# Toolforge Privacy Policy

**Effective date: September 26, 2026**

Toolforge is an Android application developed by **Fernlight**. Toolforge provides tools for working with photos, videos, audio, PDFs, GIFs, archives, and other supported files.

This Privacy Policy explains what information Toolforge accesses, collects, uses, and shares, how your files are handled, and how information is retained and protected.

## 1. Information We Collect

Toolforge does not require you to create a user account.

Toolforge is designed primarily for local processing. Files selected for processing are processed directly on your Android device by the relevant tools whenever local processing is supported.

Toolforge does, however, use third-party services for application analytics and purchase/subscription management. These services may process limited technical, usage, purchase, and subscription-related information.

### Analytics information

Toolforge uses **PostHog** for application analytics and product improvement.

Depending on the information generated by the application, analytics events may include:

* The Toolforge feature or tool used
* Application screen or feature interactions
* Whether an operation completed, failed, or was cancelled
* Processing duration or other performance information
* Application version
* Operating-system or device-related technical information associated with an analytics event
* Pseudonymous identifiers used to distinguish analytics events or installations

Analytics are used to understand how Toolforge is used, diagnose technical problems, measure feature usage, improve performance, and improve the application.

Toolforge does **not intentionally send the contents of your photos, videos, audio files, PDFs, documents, or other processed files to PostHog**.

### Purchase and subscription information

Toolforge uses **Google Play Billing** and **RevenueCat** to provide and manage optional Pro purchases and subscriptions.

Information associated with purchases or subscriptions may include:

* Purchase or subscription status
* Product or entitlement information
* Purchase history
* Transaction information
* Subscription renewal or expiration information
* Purchase timestamps
* Google Play purchase information or purchase tokens used to verify purchases
* A pseudonymous or anonymous RevenueCat App User ID
* Other limited technical information required to provide purchase and entitlement-management functionality

Toolforge does not intentionally send the contents of your files to RevenueCat.

Toolforge does not directly collect or store your payment-card number, bank-account credentials, UPI credentials, payment-card security code, or other payment credentials.

Payment credentials are handled by Google Play and its payment systems.

## 2. Files and Media

Toolforge is designed to process supported files locally on your device.

Files that you select for processing may include:

* Photos
* Videos
* Audio files
* PDFs
* GIFs
* ZIP and other archive files
* Other supported documents and media

When a tool supports local processing, the selected file is processed on your Android device and is not uploaded to a Toolforge server for processing.

Toolforge does not intentionally transmit the contents of your selected files to PostHog or RevenueCat.

Some Android system components and libraries may access information necessary to perform the operation requested by you.

## 3. File Access

Toolforge accesses files when necessary to perform an operation that you request.

When you select a file using Android's file or media selection interface, Android grants Toolforge access to the item you selected.

Toolforge does not intentionally access your entire photo library, contacts, messages, or unrelated personal files simply because the application is installed.

Access to files is limited to what is necessary for the relevant functionality.

## 4. File Metadata

Some Toolforge features read metadata contained within files in order to perform requested operations.

Depending on the file type, metadata may include:

* File format
* File size
* Image or video dimensions
* Creation or modification information
* Camera or device information
* Location metadata, if present
* Other embedded technical metadata

When the relevant feature performs processing locally, this metadata is processed locally on your device.

Toolforge does not intentionally transmit metadata contained in your files to the developer, PostHog, or RevenueCat.

Toolforge's metadata-removal functionality can remove supported metadata from an output file.

## 5. Internet Connectivity

Toolforge may use an internet connection for:

* Application analytics through PostHog
* Purchase and subscription management through RevenueCat
* Google Play Billing and purchase verification
* Other online services that may be required by a particular future feature

Internet connectivity is not required for local file processing when the relevant Toolforge feature does not otherwise require an online service.

Toolforge does not upload your files to a Toolforge server for local file-processing operations.

## 6. PostHog Analytics

Toolforge uses **PostHog** to collect application usage and performance analytics.

PostHog may process information associated with analytics events, including pseudonymous identifiers and technical information associated with those events.

The purpose of this processing is to:

* Understand feature usage
* Identify application errors and technical problems
* Measure application performance
* Improve Toolforge
* Understand how users interact with the application's features

Toolforge does not intentionally use PostHog to collect the contents of files processed by Toolforge.

PostHog information is retained according to the retention configuration applicable to the Toolforge PostHog project and PostHog's applicable policies.

## 7. Google Play Billing

Toolforge offers optional Pro features through Google Play Billing.

Google Play processes purchases and payment transactions associated with purchases made through Google Play.

Toolforge does not directly receive or store your payment credentials such as:

* Credit-card numbers
* Debit-card numbers
* Bank-account credentials
* UPI credentials
* Payment-card security codes

Toolforge may receive purchase-related information from Google Play Billing that is necessary to determine whether a purchase or subscription is valid and to provide the corresponding Pro entitlement.

Google handles payment information according to Google's applicable privacy practices and terms.

## 8. RevenueCat

Toolforge uses **RevenueCat** to manage Pro purchases, subscriptions, and entitlements.

RevenueCat helps Toolforge:

* Verify purchases
* Manage subscription status
* Manage Pro entitlements
* Determine whether Pro features should be available
* Maintain purchase and subscription state across supported application sessions

RevenueCat may process information required for these purposes, including:

* Purchase history
* Product information
* Subscription information
* Entitlement information
* Transaction information
* Google Play purchase information or purchase tokens
* Purchase and subscription dates
* An anonymous or pseudonymous App User ID
* Limited technical information associated with the application or device where required by the RevenueCat SDK

RevenueCat's Google Play Data Safety guidance identifies **purchase history** as information that developers using its SDK should account for in their Google Play disclosures.

Toolforge does not intentionally send the contents of your photos, videos, audio files, PDFs, documents, or other processed files to RevenueCat.

Toolforge does not intentionally send your payment credentials to RevenueCat.

RevenueCat's processing is subject to RevenueCat's own privacy policy and applicable terms.

## 9. Pro Entitlements and Local Application Data

Toolforge may store certain application state locally on your device to provide its functionality.

This may include:

* Pro entitlement state
* Subscription state or related local state
* Daily usage information
* Application preferences
* Theme preferences
* Other settings required for the application to function

Local application data is stored using Android's application storage mechanisms.

Local entitlement information does not contain your payment credentials.

## 10. Daily Usage Limits

Some Toolforge features may have usage limits for users who do not have the relevant Pro entitlement.

Information necessary to enforce applicable local usage limits may be stored on the device.

The contents of files processed by Toolforge are not required to be uploaded to a Toolforge server to enforce these local usage limits.

## 11. Data Sharing

Toolforge does not sell or rent your personal information.

Toolforge does not share the contents of your processed files with advertisers or data brokers.

Limited information may be processed by third-party service providers when required to provide Toolforge's functionality:

### PostHog

Used for application analytics, performance measurement, and product improvement.

### RevenueCat

Used for purchase, subscription, and entitlement management.

### Google Play

Used for application distribution, Google Play Billing, and purchase processing.

These providers may process information according to their respective privacy policies, terms, and service agreements.

Toolforge may also disclose information where reasonably necessary to:

* Comply with applicable law or lawful legal requests
* Prevent fraud or abuse
* Protect the security of Toolforge or its users
* Protect the rights or property of Fernlight
* Respond to legally valid requests from authorities

## 12. Data Retention

Different categories of information may be retained for different periods.

### Files

Toolforge does not maintain a server-side copy of files that are processed locally.

Files generated by Toolforge are stored on your device or in a storage location selected or controlled by you.

### Local application data

Local application data remains on your device until it is deleted by you, removed through Android's application-data controls, or removed as part of uninstalling the application, subject to Android's backup and system behavior.

### PostHog analytics

Analytics information may be retained according to the retention configuration of the Toolforge PostHog project and PostHog's applicable policies.

### RevenueCat and purchase information

Purchase, subscription, entitlement, and transaction-related information may be retained by RevenueCat and/or Google for the periods required to provide the service, maintain purchase records, prevent fraud, resolve disputes, comply with legal obligations, and satisfy applicable accounting or financial requirements.

Retention periods may therefore differ between services and may be affected by applicable laws and service configurations.

## 13. Data Deletion

Toolforge does not require or maintain a user account containing your files.

You can:

* Delete files generated by Toolforge using your device's file-management functionality
* Clear Toolforge's local application data through Android settings
* Uninstall Toolforge to remove its local application data, subject to Android's backup and system behavior
* Manage your Google Play subscriptions through your Google account

Information already processed by third-party providers such as PostHog, RevenueCat, or Google Play may be subject to those providers' own retention and deletion policies.

If you have a privacy-related request concerning information associated with Toolforge, you can contact Fernlight at **[hello.fernlight@gmail.com](mailto:hello.fernlight@gmail.com)**.

## 14. No User Account

Toolforge does not currently provide user account registration or login.

Therefore, Toolforge does not maintain:

* A Toolforge username
* A Toolforge account password
* A Toolforge user profile
* A server-side account containing your uploaded files

Purchase and subscription information may nevertheless be associated with anonymous or pseudonymous identifiers used by RevenueCat and Google Play for purchase management.

Because Toolforge does not provide account creation, there is no Toolforge user account that needs to be deleted.

## 15. Advertising

Toolforge is currently an **ad-free application**.

The current version does not use advertising networks or display third-party advertisements.

Toolforge does not sell user information to advertisers.

## 16. Children's Privacy

Toolforge is not specifically directed toward children.

Toolforge does not intentionally collect names, addresses, telephone numbers, contacts, or similar direct personal information from children.

Toolforge does not require users to create accounts or provide personal profiles.

If you believe that personal information belonging to a child has been provided to Toolforge inappropriately, please contact Fernlight at the email address provided below.

## 17. Security

Toolforge uses Android's application sandbox and platform security mechanisms to help protect locally stored application data.

The application's local-processing design reduces the need to transmit user files to remote servers.

Information transmitted to third-party services is protected using security mechanisms provided by those services, including encryption in transit where supported.

However, no method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Users should also use the security features provided by their Android device and maintain appropriate backups of important files.
